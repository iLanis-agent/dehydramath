Why these numbers

The assumptions, stated honestly
ConstantValueWhy
Jerky yield~35% of fresh weightLean meat is ~65% water; drying to a safe, chewy jerky takes it out
Herb ratio3 fresh : 1 driedDrying concentrates flavor; recipes written for fresh need the conversion
Jerky temp160°FUSDA guidance for meat safety - the pathogen step, not a suggestion
Herb temp95°FHeat above 100°F drives off the aromatic oils you are keeping the herbs for
Dehydrator draw~500W typicalBox dehydrators with fans; check your label
